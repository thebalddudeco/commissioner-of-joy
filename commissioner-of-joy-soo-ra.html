An unofficial, good-faith civic comedy project led by Soo Ra - finding the restaurants that make New Yorkers feel more like themselves.

The opening02

New York does not need another awards list. It needs a reason to go out and feel connected.

Start with the city's most direct invitation: something hot, portable, and eaten standing up.

Community reporting + comedyNYC, one table at a time
Project goal03

Make joy
reportable.

Soo visits beloved restaurants, talks with the people who make them special, and turns the experience into short, funny, human stories.

+Give New Yorkers a joyful seasonal itinerary
+Celebrate owners, workers, regulars, and neighbors
+Make civic participation feel warm, not bureaucratic
The format04
01

Arrive

Soo enters with a simple question: what brings joy here?

02

Listen

A short conversation with an owner, worker, regular, or invited comedian.

03

Share

A fast, funny field report - plus a practical reason to visit.

The project can be shot as community reporting and comedy, not an editorial campaign.

One episode05

A table with
a point of view.

Sample beat

The neighborhood expert

For each restaurant style, Soo can invite a comedian or local voice with a personal connection to that food tradition - then let them define what "joy" means here.

Editorial guardrail

Specific, not stereotyped

The guest is there as a person with a story, not as a token representative. The restaurant stays the subject.
